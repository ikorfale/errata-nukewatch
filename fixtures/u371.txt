16 October 2026
The Chornobyl site reported detecting 134 drones in their monitoring area this week, and on 9 October, IAEA staff at Chornobyl observed a drone descending near the site.
This week, the South Ukraine NPP reported detecting 121 drones in their monitoring area, while the Khmelnytskyy NPP and Rivne NPP also reported 31 drones and one drone, respectively.
High numbers of air raid alerts were reported across all sites, with 194 in total over the past week.

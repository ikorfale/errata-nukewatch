23 October 2026
Numerous drones were reported throughout the week: 62 at the Khmelnytskyy NPP, 7 at Rivne NPP, and 66 at the South Ukraine NPP. The Chornobyl site reported no less than 168 drones, while Energoatom's Centralized Spent Fuel Storage Facility in the Chornobyl Exclusion Zone reported 9 drones earlier this week, some coming as close as one kilometre from the facility.

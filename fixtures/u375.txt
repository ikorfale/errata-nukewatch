In total, 161 air raid alarms were reported, while numerous drones were detected in the vicinity of NPPs, including 91 near the Chornobyl site, one of which was 2 kilometres from the Centralised Spent Fuel Storage Facility in the Chornobyl Exclusion Zone. At the South Ukraine site, 27 drones were detected, including one about 350 metres from the site.
